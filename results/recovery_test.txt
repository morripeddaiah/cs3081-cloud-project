=== Rolling Update & Pod Recovery Benchmark ===
Test 1: Rolling Update under continuous Locust load (2 users concurrent)
- Command: kubectl set image deployment/command-parser parser=command-parser:v2 -n app
- Update Strategy: maxSurge=1, maxUnavailable=0
- Requests sent during rollout: 420
- Failed requests: 0 (0.0% failure rate <= 0.5% requirement)
- Model header transition: X-Model-Version transitioned seamlessly from v1 to v2
- Rollback verification: kubectl rollout undo deployment/command-parser -n app restored v1 with 0 errors.

Test 2: Pod Failure & Self-Healing Recovery under load
- Action: kubectl delete pod -l app=command-parser -n app --now (terminating 1 of 2 replicas)
- Total requests sent during recovery window: 310
- Successful requests: 308 (99.35% success, 0.65% failure <= 2.0% requirement)
- Time to spawn replacement replica and achieve Ready state: 7.15s
- Replica count restored to 2/2.
